    Calculation & salary-band assumptions

    Annual USD cash cost: salary + annual bonus; hourly × editable annual hours (default 2,080); contractor monthly rate × 12. All open roles are annualized as if staffed for a full year. Benefits, employer taxes, equity, overtime and start-date proration are excluded. This is a planning run rate, not actual year-to-date spend.

    Illustrative base bands: engineering $100k–$155k; senior engineering $135k–$185k; staff/principal $155k–$225k; managers $120k–$190k; directors $180k–$270k; VPs $230k–$340k; chief officers $260k–$420k; executive leadership $350k–$600k; professional roles $75k–$120k; administrative $55k–$85k; technical/production $26–$46/hour; interns $25–$38/hour. Legal $155k–$220k; commercial $105k–$165k. Unmatched titles use $80k–$120k and are flagged for review. Title matches are approximate; classification is editable, not a legal exempt/non-exempt determination. Salary bonus assumptions range from 5% to 30% by title. Agency/contractor roles use an illustrative monthly equivalent with 15% uplift.

    Default company budgets are fully staffed demo payroll + 8%, rounded up to $10,000. Child percentages are based on initial payroll weights with deterministic variation to demonstrate over/under budget conditions. Remaining parent budget reserves direct staffing or stays unallocated. Payroll comparisons always include the department and all descendants, even when expanded. Do not add overlapping parent and child rollups. Company filters use employing company. Historical Inactive and held/gated/pending roles are excluded. Estimated department assignments affect payroll; review before relying on them.

    Edits stay in this browser page. Save a scenario before closing, then reopen the same workbook and load the scenario to restore pay, budgets and assignment edits. No workbook compensation fields are read or overwritten.
